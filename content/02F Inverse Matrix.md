The **Inverse Matrix** $A^{-1}\in M_{n}$ is the matrix that, for a matrix $A\in M_{n}$:

$$
A^{-1}A=AA^{-1}=I_{n}
$$

The inverse gives us the ability to solve equations that have a matrix $A$ as coefficient to another matrix.
Say $AX=B$, then if $A^{-1}$ exists, we can do $X=A^{-1}B$ to find $X$.

# Finding the inverse

Before any search for the inverse, **first prove det(A) != 0**.

The equation that needs to be solved is $AX=I$.
